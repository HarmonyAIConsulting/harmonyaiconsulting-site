AI Receptionist for Brunswick County & Horry County Businesses

Most local businesses miss calls while they're on a job or serving customers. An AI receptionist picks up when you can't. I set it up for service businesses in Brunswick County, NC and Horry County, SC.

What does the AI receptionist do?

  • Answers calls 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including nights and weekends.
  • Takes the caller's name, phone number and what they need, and passes it to you.
  • Books appointments the way you take them, set up with you during onboarding.
  • Sends a missed-call text-back so a caller you couldn't answer still hears from you.
  • Tells every caller up front that it's an AI assistant.

Who is it for?

Local service businesses that lose jobs when nobody picks up: contractors, HVAC companies, plumbers, roofers and restaurants.

How does the missed-call text-back work?

When a call goes unanswered, the receptionist can text the caller so they are not lost. Texting needs a carrier registration for your business. I handle that with you, and I'll tell you plainly where it stands before it is turned on.

What does it not do?

It does not make promises for you. It sticks to the answers you give it, it won't quote a price or promise a job you haven't approved, and it passes anything else to you.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your business and how many calls you get. The free 15-minute assessment gives you a plain number, with no obligation.
