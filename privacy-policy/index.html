Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Harmony AI Automation Media & Branding LLC ("Harmony AI," "we," "us"), which also operates Brunswick County Index and Brunswick Permit Radar, collects, uses and protects information when you visit harmonyaiconsulting.com or brunswickcountyindex.com, call or text us, chat with our website assistant, or buy our services.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: name, business name, email, phone number, website, service area, business details, photos and logos you upload, and anything you write in a form, chat or message.
  • Calls, texts and chats: when you call or text (910) 294-5771 or use the website chat, our AI assistant (HALI) answers. We keep the messages, call details and transcripts so we can follow up and improve service.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number.
  • Basic website data: pages visited, device and browser type, and approximate location. We may use privacy-friendly analytics to collect this. We do not sell this data.

2. How we use information

  • To answer questions, book appointments and follow up on requests.
  • To provide, bill for and support the services you buy.
  • To review and publish business listings on Brunswick County Index.
  • To send the text messages and emails you agreed to receive.
  • To keep our sites secure and improve them.
  • To meet legal obligations.

3. Text messaging (SMS) privacy

If you give us your mobile number and opt in, we use it only to send the messages you agreed to (for example, replies to your inquiry, appointment and service updates, and, if you separately agreed, offers and local updates).

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors in support services, such as customer service and text message delivery, is permitted. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, and only as needed to do their job:

  • Stripe (payments)
  • Twilio (phone calls and text messages)
  • Google Workspace (email, calendar, forms and file storage)
  • Web3Forms (website form delivery)
  • Netlify and Cloudflare (website hosting, security and analytics)
  • Oracle Cloud and AI model providers that power our assistant HALI (to process calls and chats)

We may also share information if required by law or to protect our rights, or as part of a sale or transfer of the business.

Public listings: information you submit for a Brunswick County Index listing (business name, phone, website, hours, description, photos) is meant to be published publicly once approved.

5. AI disclosure

Our phone line and website chat are answered by an AI assistant, HALI. HALI may make mistakes. Prices and terms on our website and in your signed order are what count. A real person (Harmony) reviews requests before any payment link is sent.

6. How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above, for active client relationships, and for tax and legal records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Ask to see, correct or delete your personal information.
  • Opt out of texts (reply STOP) or emails (unsubscribe link or email us).
  • Ask us to remove or update your Brunswick County Index listing.

Email harmony@harmonyaiconsulting.com or call (910) 294-5771. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections and trusted providers. No system is 100% secure. Never send passwords or login details through our forms, chat or texts.

9. Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
