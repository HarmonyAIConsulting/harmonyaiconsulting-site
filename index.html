Questions, plain answers.

What does "Get Found" mean now?

It means customers can find you on Google and in AI answers from tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Meta AI. Google comes first: your website, your Google Business Profile and your reviews. Then I make sure the same facts about your business are clear and consistent everywhere AI tools look.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT or Google will recommend my business?

No. Nobody can honestly promise a ranking or an AI recommendation. What I do is make your information accurate, consistent and easy for search engines and AI tools to read, and ask every customer for a Google review. Then I check what the AI tools say about you each month and show you.

Do I have to sign a long contract, and are there other costs?

The first term is 3 months, then it's month to month with 30 days' notice. There's a one-time setup fee of $297 ($147 at the founding rate). You pay your own phone and texting fees. Extras for Get Found or Get Seen: a custom jingle ($397 one-time) and the AI phone agent on its own ($197 a month + $297 setup).

Who owns my website, reviews and phone number?

You do. You always own your Google Business Profile, your reviews, your phone number and your contacts.

Do you only send happy customers to Google?

No. I ask every customer for a Google review. An unhappy customer can tell you first so you can make it right, but nobody is ever blocked from posting.
